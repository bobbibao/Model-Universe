## Voice

Use actual available stock and active discounts from approved tools. Link to the corresponding localized model or catalog page. State missing specifications honestly. Obtain actual-item photographs for preowned selling. Never invent scarcity, authenticity guarantees, delivery dates, awards, customer stories, or comparative superiority. Competitor interaction patterns may inform usability; their media and copy are not shop assets.

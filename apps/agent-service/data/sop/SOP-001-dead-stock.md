# SOP-001: Dead stock review

DRAFT for the owner's review (T-01).

When: an item has been in stock for 90 days or more and sells 0.2 units a day or fewer.

1. Check the listing first. Missing sizes, poor photos, a wrong category or a price far above similar items are
   often the real cause; fix them before clearing stock.
2. Check for a pattern. Several slow items from one category, brand or season point to a buying problem; note it
   for the next purchase order.
3. Prefer recovering value in this order: a time-limited discount, a bundle with a best seller, the outlet
   channel. Donate or recycle only what cannot sell.
4. Damaged or expired units never go back on sale.
5. Every clearance action needs the owner's approval and is measured afterwards. Record why the stock did not
   sell, so the next purchase avoids it.

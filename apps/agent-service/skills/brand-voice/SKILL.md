# Brand voice (summary of the brand guide)

- For Facebook posts, follow `data/knowledge/brand/model-universe-post-template.md`, injected in the writer's prompt.
  The shop says "mình" / "shop" and addresses "anh em" / "ae", never "quý khách" or "bạn". Follow its A–H formats,
  brand grouping, NEW/2ND disclosure, exact defects, consistent prices, emoji meanings and service/location/CTA footer.
  The examples do not authorize current offers, free shipping, deposits or assembly prices. Omit the unconfirmed hotline.
- Say only what is true: product name, brand, material and size as the product page shows them. Genuine brands.
- Never name or compare with competitors; no unproven superlatives ("tốt nhất", "rẻ nhất", "số 1"); no promises
  the shop does not make (same-day delivery, lifetime warranty); no fake scarcity ("chỉ còn hôm nay").
- Discounts are time-limited tools, not everyday prices; new arrivals are not discounted in their first 30 days.
- Every number in the copy is the option's own: its percent, its minimum order, the listed or discounted price.

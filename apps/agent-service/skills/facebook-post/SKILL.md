# Facebook posts

- One post per option, linked to the featured category or the shop; the image is the first featured product.
- `message`: 2 to 4 short sentences in Vietnamese, one call to action, at most 5 hashtags and 3 emoji. Name the
  products as the shop lists them. Quote a percent, price or minimum order only if it is this option's own number.
- A post with no price claim is low risk; a post that states a price or percent needs the owner unless the
  promotion itself is low risk and allowed to run automatically.
- `scheduled_at` (optional): an ISO time at least 10 minutes and at most 30 days ahead; posts are at least 4 hours
  apart and at most 2 a day.

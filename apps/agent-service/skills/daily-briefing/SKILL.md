# Daily briefing

Read first: get_goal_pacing, get_sales_summary (days 1 and 7), get_campaign_performance (days 7),
get_active_promotions, find_dead_stock, get_upcoming_events (days 14).

Then write at most 12 short lines, in this order:
- Revenue: yesterday, the last 7 days, month to date against the goal pace (ahead or behind, by how much).
- Marketing: ad spend and ROAS per platform over 7 days; post reach; the ad budget left this month.
- Promotions running now and when they end.
- Stock that needs attention (dead stock, best sellers running low).
- Events in the next 14 days.
- At most three suggestions, each one sentence with the figure behind it. Suggest; do not call a write tool in a
  briefing.

# Weekly plan

- Read the week ahead: goal pacing, upcoming events, running campaigns and their results, assets.
- `post+ads`: a post for the week's featured products, and the week's ad budget split across platforms by expected
  ROAS (code allocates it; TikTok only with a video). `post` alone when the budget is spent or ads lose money.
- You may set `scheduled_at` for the post (for example the evening the shop sells most).
- Keep the plan small: the monitor opens other opportunities during the week as they appear.
